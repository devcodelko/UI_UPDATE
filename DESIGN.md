## Brand & Style
This design system expresses the institutional dignity, governance, and forward-looking industrial vigor of a premier state consultancy. It bridges the strategic analytical rigor of global management firms with the historical stability and sovereignty of state-backed economic development. 

The aesthetic style is **Corporate / Modern** anchored in high-contrast editorial clarity, structured geometry, and disciplined hierarchy:
- **Trust & Authority:** Pure, luminous surfaces anchored by deep slate typography deliver clarity and gravitas across complex institutional workflows.
- **Strategic Dynamism:** Precise accents of deep cyan/teal and radiant terracotta evoke technological modernization balanced with heritage.
- **Architectural Discipline:** Micro-radii (8px), fine slate framing lines, and rhythmic data structures build an atmosphere of institutional stability and operational precision.

## Colors
The color architecture derives directly from the enterprise emblem's primary duel of cyan and warm terracotta:
- **Primary Cyan (`#138D9E`):** Represents strategic vision, state governance, clarity, and institutional modernization. Used for primary navigation, critical interactive states, key charts, and authoritative focal points.
- **Secondary Orange (`#E06714`):** Represents kinetic momentum, industrial growth, and entrepreneurship. Deployed purposefully for key calls to action, pending milestones, highlights, and primary alerts.
- **Tertiary Teal (`#0E7490`):** Provides depth and tonal weight for headers, high-contrast badges, data density indicators, and pressed states.
- **Neutral Slate (`#1E293B`):** The structural bedrock for headlines, editorial typography, and high-contrast boundaries.

### Functional Surfaces
- **Canvas Base:** `#FFFFFF` (Crisp bright white canvas)
- **Container Surfaces:** `#F8FAFC` (Slate subtle tint) and `#F4F8F8` (Cyan-infused atmospheric layer)
- **Structural Dividers:** `#E2E8F0` and `#CBD5E1` for precise tabular layouts and card perimeters.

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans provides geometric authority with subtle humanist warmth. 

- **Display & Headlines:** Set in bold and extrabold weights with negative letter tracking to mirror high-end financial and management publication headers.
- **Data & Tables:** Body copy relies on neutral 400 weights with generous line-heights to support heavy industrial reporting, RFP specifications, and analytical figures.
- **Labels & Subtitles:** Micro-labels and institutional badges leverage uppercase styling, semi-bold to bold weight, and extended letter spacing (`+0.05em`), directly referencing the industrial subtitle found in the corporate emblem.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a balanced 12-column grid system calibrated for enterprise management dashboards, project tenders, and formal advisory portals.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with 1.5rem (`24px`) gutters and 2rem (`32px`) external page margins, maximum container width capped at 1440px for comfortable reading lines.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns with 1rem (`16px`) gutters and 1.5rem (`24px`) margins. Sidebar transforms into a collapsible rail.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with 0.75rem (`12px`) gutters and 1rem (`16px`) canvas margins. Tabular content shifts into stacked metric cards.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is conveyed through crisp outlines and light, tinted atmospheric shadows rather than heavy skeuomorphism. This enforces an executive, clean-room consultation feel.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Surface white (`#FFFFFF`) or tinted slate (`#F8FAFC`) with no shadow. Structural integrity is defined by a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Level 1 (Card / Resting Tile):** 1px solid border (`#E2E8F0`) paired with an ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(14, 116, 144, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover / Floating Module):** Raised border (`#CBD5E1`) paired with `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(19, 141, 158, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal / Executive Dialog):** High-level overlay backed by a soft dark backdrop (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.45)`) and depth shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.15), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(19, 141, 158, 0.06)`.

## Shapes
The shape system utilizes a disciplined 8px (`0.5rem`) standard base radius. 

- **Core Elements:** Buttons, inputs, dropdowns, and data badges apply the standard 8px radius, balancing modern software ergonomics with corporate solidity.
- **Surface Panels & Cards:** Medium and large structural containers leverage 12px to 16px corner radii to maintain a clean editorial enclosure.
- **Status Indicators & Circular Badges:** Kept full-round (pill-shaped) exclusively for numeric notification pips, identity avatars, and progress chips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Institutional):** Deep Cyan background (`#138D9E`), white text, 8px radius, bold tracking. On hover: darkened cyan (`#0E7490`) with soft depth shadow.
- **Accent (Action/Submission):** Warm Tangerine background (`#E06714`), white text. Reserved for primary operational conversions and final milestone submissions.
- **Secondary (Outline):** Pure white fill, 1px border in slate (`#CBD5E1`), text in neutral charcoal (`#1E293B`). On hover: background shifts to `#F4F8F8` with primary cyan border.
- **Text / Ghost:** Transparent background with `#138D9E` text; used for tertiary navigation.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Height standard is 44px with 8px radius.
- Background: pure white with a 1px `#E2E8F0` border.
- Focus state: border transforms to `#138D9E` with a 3px outer glow ring (`rgba(19, 141, 158, 0.15)`).
- Error state: border shifts to Crimson (`#DC2626`) with accompanying caption icon.

### Cards & Analytical Panels
- White background (`#FFFFFF`) with 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) and 8px to 12px corner radius.
- Headers feature a subtle bottom border (`#F1F5F9`) separating metadata from body content.
- KPI summary cards feature a 3px top accent bar in either Primary Cyan or Tangerine Orange to convey operational categorization.

### Status Badges & Chips
- **Active / Verified:** Soft cyan background (`rgba(19, 141, 158, 0.10)`) with solid cyan text (`#0E7490`).
- **Under Review / Priority:** Soft orange background (`rgba(224, 103, 20, 0.12)`) with deep terracotta text (`#C2530A`).
- **Complete / Compliant:** Emerald tint with forest green text.
- Form factor: Pill radius (full round) with 4px vertical / 10px horizontal padding.

### Data Tables & Governance Lists
- Alternating row styling utilizing pure white and `#FAFAFA`.
- Header row set in subtle slate `#F8FAFC` with bold uppercase column identifiers (`label-sm`).
- Cell separators use thin 1px `#E2E8F0` borders for optimal legibility during large-scale audit and compliance reviews.
