Role Overview

At Varion Advisors Analytics (VAA), we engineer state-of-the-art enterprise platforms, data analytics solutions, and human-centric digital experiences. We are looking for an innovative professional who thrives at the intersection of clarity, design excellence, and scalable technology to craft impactful interfaces for both government and corporate initiatives.

Key Responsibilities

Conceptualize and craft intuitive user experiences, interactive prototypes, and production-ready design systems using Figma and Adobe Creative Suite.

Collaborate with cross-functional product managers, data analysts, and front-end engineering teams to translate complex workflow requirements into frictionless user interfaces.

Conduct user research, journey mapping, and usability testing to ensure design accessibility and optimal engagement across desktop and mobile devices.

Develop engaging branded visual assets, dashboards, marketing collateral, and digital studio graphics adhering to Varion brand guidelines.

Required Qualifications & Skills

Bachelor's or Master's degree in Design, Computer Science, Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), or equivalent hands-on experience.

Demonstrated mastery of Figma, Adobe XD, Photoshop, Illustrator, and rapid wireframing & prototyping tools with a strong portfolio.

Solid grasp of typography, layout composition, responsive design constraints, and accessibility standards (WCAG 2.1).

Strong analytical problem-solving abilities and effective communication skills to articulate design decisions to executive stakeholders.
